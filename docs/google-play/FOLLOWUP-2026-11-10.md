## R2 upload/deletion race

Official R2 documentation says conditional PutObject headers are checked
atomically at object commit. A missing HEAD response does not cancel an earlier
in-flight write. Sources checked 2026-11-10:

- https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/api/s3/extensions/
- https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/reference/consistency/
- https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/api/s3/presigned-urls/

The new **default-off** `R2_WRITE_ONCE_UPLOADS_ENABLED` path mints only server
generated `uploads/{image-upload-UUID}/sealed-v1/image.{jpg,png,webp}` keys and
signs `If-None-Match: *`. Account erasure replaces these objects with a permanent
zero-byte content-free guard and verifies its metadata; it must not delete the
guard. This prevents earlier conditional PUTs from committing image data after
erasure. It does not prove absence of every object: the opaque random key and
zero-byte guard remain. Legacy keys have no such signed condition and stay
pending rather than being falsely declared erased.

New records must bind their original storage namespace (account/endpoint/bucket)
to the durable erasure manifest. Credentials may rotate without changing that
namespace. Missing or mismatched provenance must not complete against a new
bucket. Guard PUT requests sign the empty-payload hash and content type using
server-only Authorization headers; they are not returned to clients.

Before enabling: verify actual R2 conditional signing, concurrency, browser CORS
allowing `If-None-Match`, and that **no bucket lifecycle/cleanup rule removes
these guards**. A lifecycle deleting guards invalidates the barrier. Keep the
feature disabled until these gates pass. Resolve pre-existing legacy uploads
through an authoritative revocation/drain process; expiry/DELETE/HEAD alone is
insufficient. Minimal guard and tombstone retention require policy disclosure.
