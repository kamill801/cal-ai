# Cal AI Mascot Concept V1

## Design Read

For Korean users in their 20s and 30s, create a small nutrition coach that feels warm and lovable during daily use without making the product childish or less trustworthy.

## Prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit

Asset type: high-fidelity Cal AI mobile dashboard mascot redesign concept

Primary request: Replace only the two existing simple round leaf-face mascots in the supplied Cal AI dashboard with one more charming, original, premium-quality small coach character. Preserve the entire dashboard layout, Korean interface text, typography hierarchy, cards, spacing, data, buttons, cream background, dark green CTA, and all other UI exactly as shown.

Input image: The supplied dashboard screenshot is the edit target and product-layout reference.

Character direction: An original warm ivory sprout-shaped mini coach with a softly asymmetric bean or seed silhouette, a complete tiny body rather than a face inside a circle, very short rounded arms and feet, one small deep-green leaf sprout, glossy dark charcoal oval eyes with tiny highlights, a small confident smile, subtle coral cheeks, and gentle soft-vinyl or clay depth. Cute enough to feel lovable but restrained enough for Korean users in their 20s and 30s to see daily. Supportive, alert, competent, and not infantile.

Character usage: The top-right mascot uses a small full-body waving pose. The bottom guidance card mascot uses a slightly larger full-body pose leaning forward and pointing toward the recommended action, with a quiet encouraging expression. Both clearly depict the same character and remain secondary accents.

Visual direction: Clean Korean consumer wellness app, refined 2.5D mascot rendering, soft warm light, crisp silhouette at small size, limited details, premium app-store-level polish.

Color palette: Warm ivory body, dark forest green leaf and tiny accessory accents, charcoal facial features, muted coral cheeks; harmonize with the existing green and cream interface.

Must keep: Original screen dimensions and composition, all visible Korean UI copy, card sizes, calorie and macro values, green CTA, no new panels or decorative clutter.

Avoid: Generic egg character, plain circular face, food with a face, robot, human child, anime, furry animal, resemblance to well-known consumer app mascots, exaggerated baby proportions, huge eyes, plastic 3D emoji look, extra gradients, extra text, logos, watermark, altered nutrition data, distorted Korean text.

## Review Notes

- Stronger full-body silhouette and pose language than the current circular face.
- Works as a supportive product accent without competing with nutrition data.
- The generated Korean text is layout-only and must not be reused as production copy.
- Next iteration should decide whether the neckerchief feels too much like a costume and whether the leaf should remain part of the identity.
