# Cal AI Mascot Action Sheet V1

## Design Read

Give Korean wellness-app users in their 20s and 30s a consistent small coach that reacts to each product moment without making the service feel childish or distracting from health data.

## Poses

1. Meal logging: eating rice with a bowl and spoon.
2. Food analysis: inspecting a meal with a magnifying glass.
3. Workout: flexing both arms with an energetic expression.
4. Progress: presenting an upward progress card.
5. Weekly coach: holding and explaining a clipboard.
6. Achievement: jumping with both arms raised.

## Identity Lock

- Warm ivory sprout-shaped body.
- Two dark-green leaves.
- Dark forest-green triangular neckerchief with a cream heart.
- Charcoal eyes, coral cheeks, and a small expressive mouth.
- Premium soft 2.5D clay or vinyl rendering.
- Exactly two arms and two legs in every pose.

## Generation Constraints

- Six full-body poses in a clean 3 by 2 landscape grid.
- No labels, logos, watermarks, UI panels, or costume changes.
- Props must remain visibly separate from limbs.
- Preserve proportions, lighting, palette, and camera angle across all poses.
- Avoid resemblance to well-known consumer-app mascots.

## Review Notes

- Anatomy check passed for all six poses: two arms and two legs each.
- The meal and workout poses communicate their page context immediately.
- The character reads clearly at small UI sizes while staying secondary to product data.
- Final implementation assets should be generated individually with transparent backgrounds after approval.
