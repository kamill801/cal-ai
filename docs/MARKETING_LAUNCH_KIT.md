# Cal AI Beta Marketing Launch Kit

Last updated: 2026-08-28

## Positioning

### One-line promise

> 사진 한 장으로 한국식 한 끼를 기록하고, 남은 단백질과 다음 운동까지 이어주는 믿을 수 있는 몸 변화 코치.

### Category

Cal AI is not positioned as another calorie counter. The product combines three jobs that users currently split across separate apps:

1. fast photo-based meal logging,
2. calorie and protein guidance that exposes uncertainty,
3. strength-training plans and weekly next actions grounded in recorded behavior.

### Primary audience

- 주 3-5회 웨이트를 하며 감량 또는 체형 개선을 원하는 20-30대
- PT를 받고 있지만 혼자 먹는 시간까지 관리하기 어려운 사람
- 국밥, 덮밥, 백반, 배달식처럼 구성과 양이 매번 달라 기록을 포기했던 직장인
- 회원의 식단 기록과 운동 수행을 한 흐름으로 확인하고 싶은 소규모 PT 코치

## Message Pillars

### 1. Korean meals, one photo

Do not lead with "AI accuracy." Lead with reduced logging effort for real Korean meals.

- Core copy: `국밥도 덮밥도, 사진 한 장이면 기록이 시작돼요.`
- Proof in product: the app identifies visible foods, shows a calorie range, and asks only the highest-impact question.

### 2. Honest estimates users can correct

The trust advantage is not pretending a photo has one exact answer.

- Core copy: `정답처럼 단정하지 않고, 근거와 범위를 보여드려요.`
- Proof in product: confidence labels, assumptions, range narrowing, and edit-before-save.

### 3. Protein and training in one loop

Calorie logging is the entry point; body change is the outcome.

- Core copy: `먹은 단백질에서 끝나지 않고, 오늘 채울 양과 다음 운동까지 연결해요.`
- Proof in product: remaining protein, workout prescription, performance logging, recovery, and weekly coaching.

### 4. A coach that gives one next action

Avoid long AI reports. Promote the product as a calm coach that prioritizes one useful action.

- Core copy: `오늘 필요한 한 가지를 먼저 알려드려요.`
- Proof in product: next-meal guidance, next workout, and weekly evidence summary.

## Channel Plan

### Weeks 1-2: recruit a narrow beta

- Recruit 20-30 users from PT studios, lifting communities, and founder-owned social accounts.
- Publish short videos that show the real scan-to-save interaction, not abstract AI visuals.
- Use KakaoTalk Channel for beta notices and structured feedback links.
- Publish one Naver post around the search intent `사진 칼로리 계산`, `단백질 섭취량`, and `헬스 식단 기록`.

### Weeks 3-4: prove repeat use

- Share anonymized patterns such as "one confirmation reduced the estimate range" without publishing personal health data.
- Publish a seven-day usage diary focused on logging time, repeat meals, and workout completion.
- Test two acquisition messages: `빠른 한국식 식사 기록` versus `식단과 웨이트를 연결하는 코치`.
- Invite micro-creators with a real lifting audience rather than broad lifestyle reach.

## Experiments And Metrics

The beta succeeds when users repeat the loop, not when a launch post gets views.

| Funnel | Primary metric | Initial learning target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acquisition | beta landing-to-install rate | compare meal-speed vs body-change message |
| Activation | first meal saved within first session | identify permission or analysis drop-off |
| Core loop | users with 3+ meal logs and 1+ workout log in 7 days | prove nutrition-to-training connection |
| Retention | day-7 active rate | compare users who used weekly coach vs those who did not |
| Trust | edit rate and `분석이 도움 됐어요` response | learn where ranges or food labels fail |
| Referral | beta invite shares per active user | test whether the result feels worth sharing |

## Claims Guardrail

Safe claims:

- `사진으로 식사 기록을 시작할 수 있어요.`
- `예상 범위와 분석 근거를 보여드려요.`
- `기록한 식사를 바탕으로 남은 칼로리와 단백질을 계산해요.`
- `목표와 운동 기록을 바탕으로 다음 운동을 제안해요.`

Do not publish:

- `가장 정확한`, `100% 정확한`, `3초 만에 정확한 칼로리`
- guaranteed weight loss, muscle gain, or body transformation claims
- medical, injury, posture, body-fat, or appearance diagnosis claims
- claims that the product replaces a trainer, dietitian, or medical professional
- body-photo AI analysis claims while the production provider remains disabled or mock

## Current Beta Disclosure

The marketing surface may show meal analysis, nutrition tracking, workout planning, performance logging, progress records, and weekly coaching. It must not promote body-photo AI assessment as a working production feature until the real provider, explicit consent, retention enforcement, and deletion flow have passed production E2E verification.
